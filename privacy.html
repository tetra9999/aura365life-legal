1. In one paragraph

Aura365Life shows you where your friends and family are, on a map, if they have chosen to share

that with you. To do this it collects your location, including while the app is closed, and shares

it with the specific people you have added to a private Circle. It never shows your individual

location to a public Group — groups only ever see anonymised crowd counts covering an area of

roughly 1.2km by 0.6km, and only when at least five people are in that area. The app is for people

aged 13 and over.

2. Who this is for, and the age limit

You must be 13 or older to use Aura365Life. We ask for your date of birth when you create an

account and refuse the account if you are under 13. We store your date of birth so we can prove the

check happened; it is never shown to other users and never copied into any record another user can

read.

Aura365Life is available in the United Kingdom only. The UK's age of digital consent is 13,

which is why 13 is the minimum here. We do not offer the service elsewhere, and the app is not

listed in any other country's store.

4. Location, specifically

This is the part that matters, so it is set out in full.

Background collection. The app collects your location while it is closed or in the background.

That is the core function — a map of where your people are is useless if it only updates while you

are looking at it. Android shows a permanent notification while this is happening. You can turn it

off at any time in your device settings, and the app keeps working in a read-only mode: you will see

others, and they will not see you.

Who sees your precise location. Only members of the private Circles you have joined. Circles

are joined by a single-use invite code; nobody can add themselves to one.

Who does not. Everyone else, including every member of every public Group you belong to.

This is enforced on our servers, not in the app: the raw location records that Groups are built from

are readable by no user at all — only by our own backend process.

How Group presence works, and why it is safe. For public Groups your location is rounded to a

fixed grid roughly 1.2km by 0.6km before it leaves your phone's calculation. A cell is published

only when at least five people are in it. Below that, nothing is published at all — not a zero,

not an approximation, nothing. We snap to a fixed grid rather than adding random noise, because

random noise averages away if somebody watches you for long enough, and a fixed grid does not.

Hiding. In friends Circles you can turn on Ghost Mode and stop sharing without telling anyone.

This is stored only on your own phone and never sent to us. In family Circles Ghost Mode is

not available, and that is stated in the app.

6. How long we keep things

  • Location history: 7 days. Deleted automatically. This is enforced by the database itself, not

by us remembering to run something.

  • Group presence: not retained. Overwritten each cycle. There is no history of where a crowd

has been, and the app is built so that such a history cannot be produced.

  • Account data: until you delete your account.
  • Ghost Mode settings: never leave your device.
  • Reports you make or that are made about you: 2 years, so that a pattern of behaviour is still

visible to us on a second or third report. Kept whether or not the report was upheld.

  • Blocks: until you remove them. A block is a setting you chose, so we do not expire it.
  • Moderation records — what action we took and why: 2 years.
  • Accounts that go unused: deleted after 3 years of inactivity, along with everything attached

to them. We will email you first.

  • Backups: 7 days. We take a daily backup of the database so that a mistake or a technical

failure cannot destroy everyone's data permanently. Backups are held for 7 days and then deleted

automatically.

**This means that when you delete something — including your whole account — a copy can still

exist in a backup for up to 7 days afterwards.** It is not visible to you, to us in normal use,

or to anyone else, and it is never used to restore an account somebody chose to delete. We are

telling you because "deleted" should mean what you think it means, and for up to a week it is

slightly less absolute than that. Backups are stored in Europe, with the same protection as the

live database.

7. Deleting your account

You can delete your account from Me → Delete my account in the app. It is immediate and

irreversible, and it removes:

  • your profile, date of birth, avatar and Aura
  • your entire location history
  • your live position in every Circle
  • your membership of every Circle and Group, and the presence records behind them
  • endorsements you received and endorsements you gave
  • your sign-in credential

Records that describe a group's activity rather than a person — for example a clan's points or

its history of events — are not deleted, because they belong to everyone else in that group and name

nobody.

Web instructions: https://tetra9999.github.io/aura365life-legal/delete-account.html

8. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access your data, to have it corrected, to have it erased, to

restrict or object to how we use it, and to receive a copy in a portable form. You can exercise all

of these by contacting tnjfleming@gmail.com, and you can delete everything yourself from inside

the app (§7).

Correcting your date of birth. If you entered it wrongly, email us and we will correct it. The

app does not let you edit it yourself, to stop the age check being worked around — but that is a

limit of the app, not a refusal, and you have the right to have inaccurate data corrected.

Complaining to us. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, email

tnjfleming@gmail.com with "Data protection complaint" in the subject. We will acknowledge it

within 30 days and tell you what we are doing about it.

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, the UK's data

protection regulator, at ico.org.uk — you can do that at any time, and you do not have to come to

us first.

We respond to any of these requests within one month. If a request is complex or you have made

several, we may extend this by up to a further two months, and we will tell you within the first

month if that happens. There is no charge unless a request is manifestly unfounded or excessive.

9. Children

We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

If you think someone under 13 has an account, email tnjfleming@gmail.com. Tell us the display

name or the email address on the account, and how you know. You do not need to prove anything, and

you do not need to be their parent — a report from anyone is enough for us to look.

What we do. We suspend access to the account while we look, so nothing further is collected.

We contact the account holder and ask them to confirm their date of birth. If they do not respond

within 14 days, or if it becomes clear they are under 13, we **delete the account and everything

attached to it** — profile, location history, memberships, the lot — and confirm to whoever

reported it that we have acted, without sharing anything about the account itself.

If we get it wrong and the account holder is 13 or over, they can tell us and we will restore

access; we would rather occasionally inconvenience an eligible user than leave an ineligible one

collecting location data.

The ICO's Age Appropriate Design Code applies to this service, and we have completed a Data

Protection Impact Assessment covering the risks of processing children's location data, the

measures that reduce them, and the risks we have accepted and why.

10. Reporting and safety

You can report a user or a group from inside the app. Reports are private: the person reported is

not told who reported them.

Reports are reviewed by the controller named at the top of this policy. We aim to review every

report within one working day, and no later than three. Where a report concerns an immediate

risk to a child, we prioritise it over everything else.

11. Decisions the app makes automatically

Some things in the clan features are worked out by the app rather than by a person: your points,

your rank in a clan, and whether a clan counts you as inactive. These are based on what you do in

the app — not on where you go, and never on anything about you as a person.

None of them decides anything that affects your legal rights or anything similarly serious, and

nothing about your account is closed, suspended or restricted automatically — a person always

looks first. If you think one of these has got something wrong, email us and we will look at it.
