Account deletion — the public page Play requires

Status: copy drafted, page NOT hosted. The URL is a Play requirement and a submission blocker.

Play requires a web-accessible page describing how to delete an account and what is deleted —

in addition to the in-app route. The web page is the load-bearing one: it must work for someone

who has already uninstalled the app, which is exactly when most people go looking. A page that

only says "open the app and tap Delete" fails that test and fails the requirement.

The content below is derived from what functions/src/deleteAccount.ts actually does, not from a

generic template. If that function changes, this page changes with it.

Page copy

```

DELETING YOUR AURA365LIFE ACCOUNT

You can delete your account at any time. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

FROM INSIDE THE APP

1. Open Aura365Life and go to the "Me" tab.

2. Choose "Delete account".

3. Confirm.

Your account and data are deleted immediately. You do not need to contact us, and we will not

ask you why.

IF YOU HAVE ALREADY UNINSTALLED THE APP

You do not need to reinstall it. Email tnjfleming@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and

ask for your account to be deleted. We will confirm once it is done.

WHAT IS DELETED

Everything that identifies you or describes where you have been:

  • Your profile: name, email, date of birth, avatar and your Aura.
  • Your location history.
  • Your live position in every circle.
  • Your presence in every group.
  • Your membership of every circle and group, and your rank in any clan.
  • Pings you have sent.
  • Endorsements you have given to other people, and endorsements other people have given you.
  • Your sign-in credentials.

If deleting your account leaves a circle with nobody in it, that circle is deleted too.

WHAT IS NOT DELETED

Public groups (clans) are not deleted when you leave them, even if you created the group and

even if you were its last member. A clan's history and points describe the group rather than any

one person, and other people may join it later. Nothing in a group identifies you once your

membership and presence are removed.

Aggregate map data — the "how busy is this area" information shown for public groups — is not

personal data and is never linked to you. It only ever exists where at least five people are

present, and it is recalculated continuously rather than stored as history, so it contains

nothing to delete.

HOW LONG IT TAKES

Deletion from the app happens immediately.

Location history is in any case deleted automatically after 7 days, whether or not you delete

your account.

QUESTIONS

tnjfleming@gmail.com

```
