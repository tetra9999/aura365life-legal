If someone is in danger

Contact the police. Do not wait for a reply from us. In the UK that is 999 in an emergency, or

101 otherwise.

This app is not a safety service and cannot be relied on as one. It shows where a phone is,

when that phone has signal, battery and permission — not where a person is. Someone can be

perfectly fine and not appear on the map.

Reporting something in the app

Report from inside the app — that is the fastest route, and it reaches the same person as email:

  • A person: tap them on the map, then Report.
  • A clan: open the clan, then Report.

Reports are private. The person you report is never told that you reported them, or by whom.

You can also block someone. Blocking is private too — they are not told, and "who has blocked

me" is a question this app never answers.

If you would rather not use the in-app route, email the address above. If a report concerns a

child's safety, say so in the subject line.

Common problems

Someone in my circle isn't showing up.

Almost always their phone rather than the app: no signal, low battery, location permission turned

off, or the app force-closed by the phone's battery saver. In a friends circle they may also have

turned on Ghost Mode, which is deliberately indistinguishable from any of the above — that is what

makes it usable. Ask them.

I'm not appearing to anyone else.

Check the app's own banner — it tells you when you are not sharing. Then check location permission

is set to Allow all the time; While using the app stops updates as soon as you lock the phone.

On Android, some manufacturers' battery optimisation will close the app in the background even

with permission granted.

My clan has no territory.

Territory only appears where at least five clan members are in the same area at the same time,

and it is never shown for fewer. This is a privacy floor, not a bug or a setting, and it cannot be

lowered on request. Roughly: you need about ten friends who live near each other, not five spread

across a town.

I can't join a circle.

Invite codes are single-use and expire after 24 hours. Ask for a fresh one.

I want to leave a circle.

Open the circle and tap Leave. It is always allowed and nobody can prevent it. In a family

circle the other members are told you left — they are not told where you are.

Your data

  • Delete your account — in the app, or see the deletion page. It works

even if you have already uninstalled.

  • Get a copy of your data, or ask us to correct something — email the address above. We will

respond within one month, as UK data protection law requires.

  • What we hold and for how long — the Privacy Policy. Short version: precise

location for 7 days, then deleted automatically; no advertising; no analytics; nothing sold to

anybody.
