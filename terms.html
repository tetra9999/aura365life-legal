2. What the service does

Aura365Life shares your location with people you choose:

  • Circles are private. Members see each other's precise, live position on a map. You join one

only with a single-use invite code.

  • Groups are public. They never show individual positions. They show anonymised crowd counts

and territory built from them.

You control what you share. You can stop sharing location at any time via your device settings, or

hide within a friends Circle using Ghost Mode. Ghost Mode is not available in family Circles,

and the app tells you so.

Aura365Life is not a safety or emergency service. It shows where a phone reported itself to be,

when it last managed to report. It can be wrong, delayed, or absent — because a battery died, a

signal dropped, a permission was withdrawn, or a phone was left behind. **Do not rely on it to know

6. Location accuracy, plainly

The app deliberately trades accuracy for battery life. Positions can be tens or hundreds of metres

out, and can be minutes old. A person shown at a place may not be there. A person not shown may

simply have a flat battery.
