2. What the service does

Aura365Life shares your location with people you choose:

  • Circles are private. Members see each other's precise, live position on a map. You join one

only with a single-use invite code.

and territory built from them.

You control what you share. You can stop sharing location at any time via your device settings, or

hide within a friends Circle using Ghost Mode. Ghost Mode is not available in family Circles,

and the app tells you so.
